# M1 — mechanism + window: execution plan

*Part of the ZUIL design record — see [architecture.md](architecture.md) §9 (roadmap).*

This doc zooms the roadmap's one-line **M1** into gated sub-steps. It exists because M1 is
the milestone where the **C ABI freezes**: the pump's shape is already constrained from three
directions at once (no FFI callback trampolines — iOS W^X; the browser frame-callback
inversion; thread-safe injection — [events.md](events.md) §1), and everything downstream
(umbilical U1+, the transport module, the non-Lua faces, the widget module) hangs off what M1
fixes. Step 0 proved the *pipeline*; M1 proves the *design*. The known risks are graded in the
events.md ledger; the unknown ones will surface where the design meets FFI reality — so the
method below is built to make every step falsifiable early, on all faces at once.

---

## Status (2026-06-14): a desktop/Lua vertical slice landed ahead of the disciplined plan

A pragmatic slice — desktop + LuaJIT only, **Zig impl only**, driven by the `grab-move`
acceptance test (`examples/grab-move/`) — implemented the **draw + input + clip/transform**
mechanism without the full ceremony below. Mapping to the sub-steps:

- **M1.2** (window + frame pump): landed, desktop only — Spike P (the Emscripten inversion) was
  **deferred** (wasm-only risk).
- **M1.3** (draw vocab): landed (`fill_rect`/`draw_rect`/`draw_line`/`clear`/`set_color`).
- **M1.4** (input snapshot + edges): landed **as accessor functions**, not a struct — which
  **sidesteps Spike E** (one of its two candidate outcomes, on one face). E stays *open*.
- **M1.6** (clip + transform): the **clip push/pop** and **2-D translation stack** landed (the
  transform fell out for free as a CPU offset); the `hot`/`active`/`focused` id-registry did
  **not** — grab-move does its own hit-test/z-order in user-space.
- **M1.0** (`c_abi.zig` split), **M1.1/M1.2 spikes as written**, **M1.5** (`event_post`/message/
  timer), and **M1.7** (a reusable button + `zuil.run()` sugar in-core) remain **not done**.

So the discipline below is the **still-authoritative plan** for completing M1 properly
(4-face smokes, dual-impl lockstep, the two spikes, the ABI freeze). The slice is a head-start
and a falsification, not a substitute. Full rationale + what it confirmed/falsified:
[architecture.md](architecture.md) §10 (2026-06-14).

## 1. Method: three rules

1. **Spike-first.** The two highest-unknown-density questions (§3) get dedicated spikes
   *before* any step that depends on their answers. A spike is allowed — expected — to
   invalidate part of the design cheaply; that is its job.
2. **Smoke-gated.** A sub-step lands only when the same smoke passes on every applicable
   face: `luajit` + `python3` always; the `-Dwasm` and `-Dandroid` builds whenever the step
   touches the pump or the ABI. Cross-face divergence is the cheapest detector of unknown
   unknowns — four consumers disagreeing about one struct is a design bug found in minutes.
3. **Soft stays soft.** Anything the events.md ledger grades ≥ 7 (struct *layout*, names,
   queue-semantics tuning) stays behind a shape that can still change — accessor functions or
   a version-checked struct — until at least two real consumers have exercised it. Freezing
   soft decisions early is how "careful" fails silently.

Plus the standing convention: **one decision-log entry per landed sub-step** in
[architecture.md](architecture.md) §10, recording not just what was decided but *what the step
falsified* — that is where unknown-turned-known gets written down.

## 2. The sub-steps and their gates

| Step | Lands | Exit criterion (the smoke) | Faces gated |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1.0** | `src/zuil.zig` (core) / `src/c_abi.zig` (veneer) split; `zuil_sdl_version` moves behind it | Step-0 smokes pass **unchanged** | all four |
| **M1.1** | **Spike E** — event struct across the FFI (no SDL) | synthetic event round-trips, fields verified | Lua, Py, wasm |
| **M1.2** | window + pump skeleton; **Spike P** — wasm inversion | window opens/closes cleanly via the pump | desktop + wasm |
| **M1.3** | draw vocabulary minimum: `clear`, `fill_rect`, present; continuous redraw | a colored rect, drawn from Lua | Lua, Py |
| **M1.4** | input: raw events + derived snapshot with edges | rect follows mouse, recolors on pressed-this-frame | Lua + wasm |
| **M1.5** | `zuil_event_post` + `message`/`timer` variants; bounded queue, fail-fast | local REPL half-smoke (below) | Lua, Py, wasm |
| **M1.6** | clip push/pop; id / hit-test / `hot`/`active`/`focused` registry | hit-test demo: two overlapping rects, correct `hot` | Lua |
| **M1.7** | `zuil.run()` sugar + first immediate-mode **button** demo | button built **purely from primitives** | Lua |

Step-by-step rationale (the *why* of the ordering):

- **M1.0 first, before any new export.** Retrofitting the one-core-two-faces split after ten
  exports exist is exactly the careless path; doing it while there is one symbol costs an
  afternoon. The Zig API is primary, the C ABI a veneer — the file layout should say so from
  the first real commit.
- **M1.1/M1.2 are the spikes** — see §3. Nothing draw- or input-shaped is built until both
  have reported.
- **M1.3 before M1.4**: a visible rect makes every later input smoke self-evident (you watch
  it react) instead of log-scraping.
- **M1.5 inside M1, not after.** Transports are post-M1, but the `message`/`timer` **enum
  slots and their semantics** (payload valid until next pump; FIFO merge; fail-fast post) must
  exist *now* — a pump shape that has to be re-cut later to admit them is the failure mode
  this whole doc exists to prevent. The gate is the **local half of events.md §5b's REPL
  smoke**: a same-process `zuil_event_post` carrying `return zuil.sdl_version()`, dispatched
  by the Lua host, `3002010` observed — the core moved a frame and never interpreted it, no
  socket required.
- **M1.7 is the fence test.** The button demo must need **zero core additions** beyond the
  primitives already landed. If writing `ui.button` in Lua forces a new export, the
  mechanism/policy line is drawn wrong — that is a design finding, not an inconvenience, and
  it goes in the decision log.
- The **2-D transform stack** (architecture §4) rides along in M1.6 only if it falls out of
  the clip work for free; otherwise it defers to M2 with the rest of the canvas machinery.
  *(graded soft — see ledger)*

## 3. The two spikes (highest unknown density)

### Spike E — the event struct across the FFI boundary (M1.1)

The event union's *direction* is firm; its *layout* is graded **8.5-soft**
([events.md](events.md) §8) — yet that layout is what LuaJIT cdata, Python ctypes, JS
cwrap/HEAP views, and the Zig face all touch. Unknowns to burn down, with **no SDL in the
build** (a `zuil_test_emit()` posts a synthetic event):

- tagged-union representation in C (discriminant + union vs flat struct with dead fields);
- alignment/size across **wasm32** (4-byte pointers) vs x86-64 vs arm64 — the same header must
  be read identically by all;
- the **borrow-until-next-pump** payload lifetime, exercised from each face's natural string
  type (Lua string, Python bytes, JS copy out of the heap);
- **the named decision this spike exits with**: raw struct exposed through the ABI (fast FFI
  reads, layout frozen forever) vs **accessor functions** (layout stays soft, one call per
  field). Decided *at spike exit from measurements*, not before — LuaJIT FFI call overhead vs
  cdata field reads is measurable in an afternoon and should be measured, not argued.

### Spike P — the pump inverted under Emscripten (M1.2)

Desktop tolerates a blocking `SDL_WaitEvent` between frames; the browser forbids it — the
frame callback owns control and ASYNCIFY is already rejected as default
([web.md](web.md) §3). A pump comfortable on desktop and unworkable inside
`emscripten_set_main_loop` is the single most likely "unknown reason" to invalidate M1
mid-flight, so the minimal window + poll loop runs under `-Dwasm` **before the draw
vocabulary exists**. This is also where "wakes a blocking wait *where one exists*" degrading
to a no-op on the web ([events.md](events.md) §3) stops being prose and becomes a tested
behaviour.

## 4. Explicitly not in M1 (the fence around the milestone)

- **M2 material**: text measurement/draw, IME/soft-keyboard, `blit_rgba`, render-targets.
- **Post-M1 diversions**: the TCP transport module, umbilical U1+, the JS/Python/C++ face
  work beyond keeping their smokes green, async/await sugar.
- **Widget-module material**: anything resembling a shipped `ui.*` library — M1.7's button is
  a *demo proving the primitives*, deliberately throwaway.
- **Mobile-tied mechanism**: gestures, lifecycle/GPU-context-loss events — they ride M2+ with
  a device to test against.

What *is* M1's even though it looks deferrable: the `message`/`timer` slots (§2, M1.5
rationale). Shape now, traffic later.

## 5. Concerns · Hypotheses · Choices

Firmness grades as in [events.md](events.md): 1 = hard … 10 = soft.

**Concerns** (designed around)
- The event struct crosses four FFIs with two pointer widths; one misaligned field is four
  divergent consumers. Spike E exists for this. *(2.0 — platform fact)*
- A desktop-shaped blocking pump may not invert into the browser frame callback; must be
  falsified in M1.2, not discovered under M1.4. *(2.0 — platform fact)*
- Freezing names/layout while they are graded soft; hence accessor-or-versioned shapes until
  two consumers exercise them. *(4.0)*

**Hypotheses** (to be proven, with where)
- Accessor functions cost little enough over LuaJIT FFI that layout can stay soft through M1
  — measured at Spike E exit.
- SDL's user-event queue is an adequate substrate for `zuil_event_post` — carried from
  events.md §8, first tested at M1.5.
- The immediate button needs zero core additions beyond the listed primitives — the M1.7
  fence test.

**Choices** (decided 2026-06-10)
- The core/veneer split lands at **M1.0**, before any new export. *(3.0)*
- **Spike-first ordering**: events-ABI then wasm-inversion, before draw/input. *(4.0)*
- **`message`/`timer` slots are M1 scope** though transports are not; gate = the local REPL
  half-smoke. *(3.5)*
- **Smoke matrix**: `luajit` + `python3` every step; `-Dwasm`/`-Dandroid` on every
  pump/ABI-touching step. *(4.5)*
- **Raw-struct vs accessors is a named open decision** until Spike E reports. *(9.0 — open on
  purpose)*
- Transform stack: in M1.6 only if free, else M2. *(8.0)*
